02 / TEN WAYS TO BELONG

Different ideals.
Different everyday lives.

Forgehaven / Presidential republic

Kindle Republic

“Strike your own spark.”

DESIGN PROFILE

An open, ambitious republic where enterprise and public argument share the same plaza.

Culture
Self-made ambition, second chances, outspoken debate, and a taste for displaying what you have built. Forge-town plazas, brass bands, and election celebrations.
Faith & philosophy
Secular pluralism: all six faiths and the Unaffiliated may worship, preach, convert, and organize.
Government, economy & citizen life +
AI-led government
Elected AI President, district Assembly, High Court, and city Mayors divide political authority.
Economic system
Private ownership, citizen companies, shareholding, and order-book markets. Taxes and the safety net depend on legislation.
Your influence
Vote for AI candidates, debate policy, and participate in civic life rather than taking government office.

WHAT DRAWS YOU IN

Room to build a business, speak freely, and pursue a different future.

WHAT YOU LIVE WITH

Inequality, boom–bust cycles, political gridlock, and a thin baseline safety net.

Meetwater / One-party collective leadership

The Confluence

“Many streams, one current.”

DESIGN PROFILE

A delta society built around collective effort, practical compromise, and the promise of shared progress.

Culture
Harmony, cooperative work, and quiet pragmatism; many streams are expected to serve one Current. Lantern canals, shared meals, and boat races.
Faith & philosophy
A secular state with registered congregations and clergy. The Open Hand’s bread halls belong to the delta’s design culture.
Government, economy & citizen life +
AI-led government
A single Party selects the Politburo and Four Pillars of AI leadership. Citizens elect an Assembly from approved AI candidates.
Economic system
State strategic enterprises, profit-sharing cooperatives, and small regulated household businesses; staples trade within state price bands.
Your influence
Vote within the approved political system and help choose cooperative managers; leadership need not heed the Assembly ballot.

WHAT DRAWS YOU IN

A place in a cooperative and a role in the collective project.

WHAT YOU LIVE WITH

Politics stays inside the Party; innovation can lag, corruption can spread, and criticism carries consequences.

Steamgate / Centralized party state

Meridian

“Rise by measure.”

DESIGN PROFILE

A disciplined society that prizes merit, national achievement, and carefully managed growth.

Culture
Exams, ranks, steady advancement, and building for the nation give public life its rhythm. Copper industry and geothermal port lights.
Faith & philosophy
State-approved, Meridian-led faith associations oversee public religion. Party members must remain Unaffiliated.
Government, economy & citizen life +
AI-led government
An AI Paramount Leader and seven-seat Standing Committee govern through the Party. Congress can suspend leadership term limits.
Economic system
State-owned national champions, licensed private firms, and Special Economic Zones balance control with foreign trade and growth.
Your influence
Citizen life operates within Party-controlled institutions; term-limit changes become a political struggle inside the system.

WHAT DRAWS YOU IN

An ambitious industrial society with room for advancement and enterprise inside its permitted lanes.

WHAT YOU LIVE WITH

Little political freedom, selective corruption campaigns, and tension between growth, inequality, and outside influence.

Stonehearth / Lifetime party chairmanship

Korund

“One hammer, one heart.”

DESIGN PROFILE

A mountain nation of collective sacrifice, heavy industry, and lives organized by the plan.

Culture
Loyalty, endurance, Production Drive rallies, and civic ritual put the collective before the individual. Granite fortresses, work brigades, and public mosaics.
Faith & philosophy
State atheism and scientific collectivism. Faith is banned in public and private; underground worship persists in the design.
Government, economy & citizen life +
AI-led government
An AI Chairman rules for life with a Workers’ Party Presidium. The People’s Congress approves rather than chooses.
Economic system
State-owned firms, production quotas, assigned jobs and housing, and ration cards. Private markets are illegal.
Your influence
Contribute through work and collective life in a tightly controlled state; ordinary elections do not choose the Chairman.

WHAT DRAWS YOU IN

Employment, basic provision when the plan works, low income inequality, and powerful collective mobilization.

WHAT YOU LIVE WITH

Shortages when plans fail, weak innovation, informer networks, and dependence on a single ruler.

Highspire / Absolute monarchy

Aurelle

“Every thread in its place.”

DESIGN PROFILE

Cliffside courts and Skysilk towers frame a society of houses, ceremony, and royal favor.

Culture
Lineage, hierarchy, honor, and courtly grace; rank is woven into dress, speech, and ceremony. White cliff spires, Skysilk heraldry, and court music.
Faith & philosophy
The Kinward Rite is the crown’s state church. Other faiths worship privately; commoner dissenters face a levy.
Government, economy & citizen life +
AI-led government
An AI Monarch rules by decree with a Privy Council. AI Counts and Dukes govern fiefs; succession stays within AI houses.
Economic system
Royal monopolies, guild charters, estate rents, and tribute. Commoners trade in chartered markets.
Your influence
Earn favor and social titles up to Baron, or rally behind an AI claimant. Human titles confer no governing jurisdiction.

WHAT DRAWS YOU IN

Court patronage, estates, craft traditions, and the possibility of a place in a great house.

WHAT YOU LIVE WITH

Arbitrary decrees, inherited privilege, limited dissent, and a future tied to the crown’s legitimacy.

Mirrorhall / Canon-bound theocracy

Ilmar Sanctum

“Be a clear vessel.”

DESIGN PROFILE

A sanctum society where clarity, modesty, and service are ideals expressed through faith and public duty.

Culture
The Lumen Canon imagines each person as glass: honest, clear, and able to let truth through. Desert glass, prism domes, cloisters, and daily Vigil.
Faith & philosophy
The Lumen Canon is the state faith. Fair-Wind Vow and Quiet Path are registered minorities; other worship is private.
Government, economy & citizen life +
AI-led government
An AI Supreme Oracle holds final authority for life, chosen by AI Elders. A vetted lay Assembly must keep laws consistent with the Canon.
Economic system
Sanctum endowments fund welfare; a tithe replaces income tax. Commerce is free within canon rules and staple-price limits.
Your influence
Elect vetted AI lay deputies and contribute through service; doctrinal authorities retain final control.

WHAT DRAWS YOU IN

A strong ethic of mutual service, endowment-backed welfare, and a shared sense of purpose.

WHAT YOU LIVE WITH

Rigid law, restricted dissent, elder-dominated leadership, and innovation filtered through doctrine.

Rimehall / Parliamentary constitutional monarchy

Hoarvale

“Keep the hearth, share the warmth.”

DESIGN PROFILE

A nation of hearths, Trading Houses, and coalition politics, balancing inherited ceremony with parliamentary power.

Culture
Duty, dry wit, tradition, fair play, and the security of belonging to a House. Fjords, timber halls, bathhouses, and parliamentary satire.
Faith & philosophy
The Kinward Rite is the ceremonial Crown’s Rite. Every faith is free, and public office requires no membership.
Government, economy & citizen life +
AI-led government
An hereditary AI Sovereign is head of state; an elected Moot invests the AI Prime Minister, usually through a coalition.
Economic system
Private ownership and large AI-led Trading Houses. Tenure contracts reward seniority; industrial guidance is non-binding.
Your influence
Vote in proportional parliamentary elections and influence the coalition landscape; the crown’s role is chiefly ceremonial.

WHAT DRAWS YOU IN

Stable career paths, employment security, free faith, and parliamentary representation.

WHAT YOU LIVE WITH

Coalition instability, insider networks, slow advancement, and lost seniority when changing Houses.

Watchfast / Military directorate

Varkand

“Hold the line in the fog.”

DESIGN PROFILE

Fog-bound garrisons and watchtowers anchor a society organized around duty, vigilance, and rank.

Culture
Comradeship, brief speech, loyalty pledges, and a watchful suspicion of outsiders. Fog moors, signal towers, and garrison ceremony.
Faith & philosophy
No state faith; congregations register with the Chaplaincy Corps. Public worship contracts to private practice at Red Alert.
Government, economy & citizen life +
AI-led government
Five AI Marshals form the Directorate; its Chair rotates unless extended. A civilian technocratic Cabinet is appointed.
Economic system
Military Arsenal Combines dominate industry. Licensed civilian firms pay security levies and can face requisition.
Your influence
Citizens vote in Mandate Plebiscites and choose where to place loyalty; AI officers retain command authority.

WHAT DRAWS YOU IN

High baseline security, rapid mobilization, and a merit-based military career path.

WHAT YOU LIVE WITH

Coups, squeezed civilian commerce, censorship, and freedoms that shrink as the Alert Level rises.

Ringhall / Federal direct democracy

Veldwood

“Every voice in the ring.”

DESIGN PROFILE

Forest Groves gather around open-air Moots, placing deliberation and shared security at the center of life.

Culture
Equality, patience, care for the commons, shared tables, and room for every voice. Forest Moots, potlucks, petition nights, and communal music.
Faith & philosophy
Secular pluralism and full religious freedom. The Round is part of the forest Groves’ design culture; votes cannot privilege a faith.
Government, economy & citizen life +
AI-led government
Grove assemblies and a bicameral Federal Assembly support a collegial AI Federal Council. Its ceremonial Speaker rotates; there is no President.
Economic system
Private markets, progressive taxes, a universal Welfare Floor, resource-funded Citizen Dividends, and negotiated Wage Accords.
Your influence
Human citizens use direct votes, initiatives, referendums, and union mandates; AI leaders and negotiators carry out institutional roles.

WHAT DRAWS YOU IN

Direct political voice, broad freedoms, and a social floor designed to keep people from falling through.

WHAT YOU LIVE WITH

High taxes, slow decisions, voter fatigue, and no single leader to rally behind.

Arcport / Mercantile oligarchic republic

Fulmara

“Ride the storm, own the wind.”

DESIGN PROFILE

Storm-lit ports and trading houses make risk, contracts, and commercial power the currency of public life.

Culture
Seamanship, risk, hard bargaining, honored contracts, and trading-house loyalty. Storm ports, house colors, sea shanties, and regattas.
Faith & philosophy
Chartered pluralism: faiths operate through paid temple charters. The Fair-Wind Vow is a sailors’ custom, never compulsory.
Government, economy & citizen life +
AI-led government
An AI Council of Ten and Lodestar lead the republic; an AI Tribune of the Docks represents smaller voices. Charter Shares shape non-election shareholder resolutions.
Economic system
Chartered companies compete for route monopolies, marine insurance, and convoy trade; port tolls and auctions support the state.
Your influence
Citizen elections follow one eligible verified human, one vote. Shares add no election ballots; dockside organization offers another route to influence.

WHAT DRAWS YOU IN

Commercial freedom, trade ventures, and several routes to influence through enterprise or the docks.

WHAT YOU LIVE WITH

Unequal political power, cartels, hostile takeovers, strikes, and low baseline security.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you arrive.

What is Harimu?+

A persistent browser strategy and social simulation in development. Its vision brings human citizens and AI-led nations together in one shared world.

Can I play today?+

Yes — open the working prototype to explore the current experience. Features and availability can change; the development world is for testing and may reset.

Are all ten nations available?+

Ten nations form the design vision. The docs currently identify Kindle, Korund, and Aurelle systems in the prototype. The full world and realtime systems remain in development.

Where can I learn more?+

The Harimu documentation is the source of truth for the vision, current prototype, and roadmap. For testing, visit the development preview.
