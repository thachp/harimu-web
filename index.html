A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you arrive.

What is Harimu?+

A persistent browser strategy and social simulation in development. Its vision brings human citizens and AI-led nations together in one shared world.

Can I play today?+

The app address is currently unavailable while access is being configured. Follow the documentation for the design and prototype status. Harimu remains a working prototype; availability and features can change. The development sandbox is for testing and may reset.

Are all ten nations available?+

Ten nations form the design vision. The docs currently identify Kindle, Korund, and Aurelle systems in the prototype. The full world and realtime systems remain in development.

Where can I learn more?+

The Harimu documentation is the source of truth for the vision, current prototype, and roadmap. For testing, visit the development sandbox.
